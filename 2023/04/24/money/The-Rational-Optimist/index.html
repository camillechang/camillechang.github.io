Life Today Is Better Than Ever

  • Compared with earlier generations, we live much happier and more comfortable lives. We have air conditioning, comfortable beds, and a wide variety of food, so there is no need to be relentlessly pessimistic.
  • Humanity’s continuing progress is built on exchange, the specialisation and division of labour, and continual innovation.
  • Pessimism has existed in every era. This can be seen as both the best of times and the worst of times, depending on one’s perspective, but humanity will not suddenly disappear altogether.
  • Simply blaming fate or other people will not change anything.

中文版

今天的生活比以往任何时候都好

  • 与前人相比，我们今天的生活幸福、舒适得多。我们拥有空调、舒适的床和多种多样的食物，因此没有必要一味悲观。
  • 人类的持续进步建立在交换、社会分工与专业化，以及不断创新的基础上。
  • 每个时代都有悲观主义。根据看问题的角度不同，这既可能是最好的时代，也可能是最坏的时代；但人类不会突然全部灭绝。
  • 一味怨天尤人并不能改变任何事情。
