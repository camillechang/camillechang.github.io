My Thoughts

The book is interesting and covers a great deal of basic knowledge about building wealth. However, accumulating the initial capital for investing often feels like the hardest part. If the sequence of events in the book is accurate, I admire the author: succeeding required him to think strategically.

Useful Excerpts and Lessons

  • Applying knowledge to your life is the secret to becoming wealthy. Use what you already have to build wealth; if you lack something, learn it.
  • You get what you focus on. If you focus too heavily on difficulties, you can no longer see any opportunities. If you want to become wealthy, do not look only at obstacles.
  • Becoming wealthy does not depend on whether you are kind, good, or hardworking; it depends on how many other people are capable of doing your job. Build irreplaceable skills.
  • Any method that lets you influence or sell to more than one person at the same time is a form of leverage. This is the basis of passive income.
  • Wealthy people always save part of their money for investment first, then spend what remains. Take a long-term view of money and benefit from compounding.

References

  • Author: https://www.peterspann.com.au/
  • Book: From Broke to Multi-Millionaire in Just 7 Years by Peter Spann

中文版

我的感想

这本书很有意思，涵盖了许多积累财富的基本知识。不过，投资的第一桶金往往最难积累。如果书中叙述的事件顺序准确，我很佩服作者：要想取胜，必须有策略地思考。

对我有用的摘录与启发

  • 把知识应用于生活，就是致富的秘诀。 用自己已经拥有的东西创造财富；如果有所欠缺，就去学习。
  • 你关注什么，就会得到什么。因此，当你过度关注困难时，就再也看不到任何机会。 想要富有，就不要只盯着困难。
  • 能否致富并不取决于你是否善良、是否是好人，或是否努力，而取决于有多少人能够胜任你的工作。 要培养不可替代的能力。
  • 任何能够让你同时影响一个以上的人，或向一个以上的人销售的方法，都是杠杆作用的体现。 这是被动收入的基础。
  • 富有的人总是先存下一部分钱用于投资，然后才消费剩余的钱。 要用长远的眼光看待金钱，发挥复利的作用。
