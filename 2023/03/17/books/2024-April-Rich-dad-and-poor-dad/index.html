Lesson One: The Rich Do Not Work for Money

  • Find ways to make money generate more money instead of working for a fixed salary and paying taxes. Financial freedom is reached when passive income covers your needs.
  • An asset puts money into your pocket. A liability takes money out of your pocket.

Lesson Two: Learn Financial Literacy

  • Real assets:
    1. Businesses that can operate normally without my presence. I own them, but other people operate and manage them. If I have to work there, it is not my business; it is my profession.
    2. Stocks.
    3. Bonds.
    4. Mutual funds.
    5. Income-generating real estate.
    6. Notes (IOUs).
    7. Royalties, such as those from music, manuscripts, and patents.
    8. Anything else that has value, generates income or has the potential to appreciate, and has a ready market.

Lesson Three: Mind Your Own Business

Lesson Four: The History of Taxes and the Power of Corporations

  • Use corporations to reduce taxes:
    1. Tax advantages. A corporation can do many things an individual cannot, such as paying expenses with pre-tax income, including car insurance, repairs, membership fees, meals, and more.
    2. Employees earn money, pay taxes, and live on what remains; corporations earn money, spend it, and pay taxes only on what remains.
  • Financial literacy helps you read financial statements and identify a business’s strengths and weaknesses.
  • Investing is what I call the science of making money generate more money.
  • Understand markets, the science of supply and demand. Whether an investment ultimately makes sense depends on current market conditions.

Lesson Five: The Rich Invent Money

  • Laws and regulations: Become familiar with laws concerning accounting and corporations, as well as state and national regulations. We must play the “game” by the rules.

Lesson Six: Work to Learn—Do Not Work for Money

  • When looking for a job, young people should consider what they can learn from it, not just how much they can earn.
  • The management skills needed for success include:
    1. Managing cash flow.
    2. Managing systems, including yourself, your time, and your family.
    3. Managing people.

Seek new ideas. To find inspiration for investments, start taking action.

中文版

第一课：富人不为钱工作

  • 想办法让钱生钱，而不是为了固定工资工作和交税。直到被动收入能够覆盖自己的需求，就实现财富自由了。
  • 资产是能把钱放进你口袋里的东西。负债是把钱从你口袋里取走的东西。

第二课：学习财务知识

  • 真正的资产：
    1. 不需我到场就可以正常运作的业务。我拥有它们，但由别人经营和管理。如果我必须在那里工作，那它就不是我的事业，而是我的职业。
    2. 股票。
    3. 债券。
    4. 共同基金。
    5. 能够产生收入的房地产。
    6. 票据（借据）。
    7. 版税，如音乐、手稿、专利的版税。
    8. 其他任何有价值、可产生收入或有增值潜力，并且有很好销路的东西。

第三课：关注自己的事业

第四课：税收的历史和公司的力量

  • 通过公司来避税：
    1. 减税优惠。公司可以做许多个人无法做的事，例如用税前收入支付开支，包括车险、修理费、会员费、餐饮费等等。
    2. 雇员挣钱、纳税，并靠剩下的钱为生；企业挣钱、花钱，并只为剩下来的钱缴税。
  • 财务知识能帮助你读懂财务报表，还能让你辨别一项生意的优势和劣势。
  • 投资，我把它称为钱生钱的科学。
  • 了解市场，它是供给与需求的科学。一项投资究竟有无意义，最终取决于当前的市场状况。

第五课：富人的投资

  • 法律规章：要熟悉有关会计、公司方面的法律，以及各州和国家的法规。我们必须按规则来进行“游戏”。

第六课：学会不为钱工作

  • 年轻人在找工作时要看能从中学到什么，而不是只看能挣多少钱。
  • 成功所必需的管理技能包括：
    1. 对现金流的管理。
    2. 对系统的管理，包括你本人、时间及家庭。
    3. 对人员的管理。

寻找新的思想。为了寻找投资的灵感，开始行动。
