English Version

First SAP-C02 Attempt

  • I scored 728; one more correct answer would have been enough to pass. The main reason was that, after finishing quickly, I did not review all the questions for anything I might have missed. I assumed I could simply retake the exam for free.
  • I took the exam in Chinese, which felt easier to read, and I could switch to English at any time, which was very convenient.
  • The real exam included some questions similar to the practice questions.
  • Exam topics included:
    • Route 53 inbound and outbound endpoints
    • Not many new services
    • RTO, RPO, backups, and cost-related considerations
  • You still need to practice more and become familiar with and understand your knowledge gaps.
  • After finishing the exam, I realized that I had not applied the voucher, so I would not receive a free retake. I was very sad.

Second SAP-C02 Attempt

On 2/1/2023, I took the exam for a second time and passed. After completing several hundred practice questions, I found that there were a few topics I could not understand at all, so I gave up on those parts.

  • I was still confused about advanced AWS networking.
  • After going through the material several times, I found that the questions were mainly about connections from on-premises environments to AWS or optimizing an existing system, while also considering cost.
  • The second attempt was harder than the first, but this time I really did finish in 2 hours and then checked every question one by one. I made sure I had done my best because, after this exam, I had taken enough AWS exams for the year.
  • After the exam, I felt relieved. Whether I passed or not, I had done my best and needed to spend time on other things.
  • To sum up, many questions felt like isolated knowledge points. Unless you actually use them, they can feel useless. The certificate sounds impressive, but it covers many services, and I think I may use at most 50% of them in my lifetime.

Resources I Used

  1. Neal Davis’s Udemy practice exams
  2. Stephane Maarek’s Udemy course
  3. Tutorials Dojo: AWS Certified Solutions Architect Professional Practice Exams 2022

中文版

第一次 SAP-C02 考试

  • 我得了 728 分，再答对一道题就能通过。主要原因是我很快做完后，没有重新检查所有题目，看看是否遗漏了什么。我以为下次可以免费重考。
  • 考试使用中文版，感觉更容易阅读，同时可以随时切换英文，非常方便。
  • 实际考试中包含了一些与平时练习题相似的题目。
  • 考试主题包括：
    • Route 53 入站和出站端点
    • 新服务不多
    • RTO、RPO、备份和成本相关的考量
  • 还是要多练习，熟悉并理解自己的知识盲点。
  • 考完后我才发现自己没有使用代金券，因此无法获得免费重考机会。非常难过。

第二次 SAP-C02 考试

2023 年 1 月 2 日，我参加了第二次考试并顺利通过。做了几百道练习题后，我发现有几个知识点怎么也理解不了，所以干脆放弃了那些部分。

  • 我仍然对 AWS 高级网络感到困惑。
  • 把材料过了几遍之后，我发现题目主要涉及从本地环境连接到 AWS，或者优化现有系统，同时还要考虑成本。
  • 第二次比第一次更难，但这次我确实在 2 小时内完成了考试，然后逐题检查了一遍。我确认自己已经尽力，因为考完这次后，我今年参加的 AWS 考试已经够多了。
  • 考完后我觉得轻松了。不管是否通过，我都已经尽力，也该把时间花在其他事情上了。
  • 总的来说，很多题感觉只是零散的知识点。除非实际用到，否则会觉得没什么用。这个证书听起来很厉害，但它涵盖了很多服务，我觉得自己一生中最多可能只会用到其中的 50%。

  2. Stephane Maarek 的 Udemy 课程
