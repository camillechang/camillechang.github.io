Reflections on Life

English Version

One

I started clearing out some old belongings and came across a diary I once kept. On October 9, 2012, I wrote that I wanted to study abroad but worried that I did not have enough money or good enough grades.

Looking back now, I used the savings from several years of work and the money I earned from part-time jobs abroad to complete my postgraduate degree. I then spent three years trying a job I loved, but the pay was so low that I eventually had to give it up and choose work related to my degree. I really wish I could tell my younger self: live in the present, keep your feet on the ground, and the things you want will slowly come true.

Two

Learn to embrace each present moment. In the end, we often discover that it is the accumulation of one moment after another that completes our past and creates our future.

中文版

一

开始清理以前的东西，翻到自己曾经的日记。2012 年 10 月 9 日这天，我写下了想去国外读书，但是没钱、没有好成绩的顾虑。

现在回头来看，靠着工作了几年的积蓄和在国外兼职，我已经读完研究生，并且尝试了三年自己喜欢的工作，最后因为薪水太低不得不放弃，而选择了专业对口的工作。真想对当时的自己说：过好当下，脚踏实地，你想要的都会慢慢实现的。

二

学会把握每个当下。到最后我们往往会发现，正是那一个又一个当下的累积，圆满了我们的过去，成就了我们的未来。
