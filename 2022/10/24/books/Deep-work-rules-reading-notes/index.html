English Version

1. Deep Work: Deep Work Rules

The Ideas

  1. Deep work is valuable.
  2. Deep work is rare.
  3. Deep work is meaningful.

The Rules

  1. Work deeply.

    In a prolonged, distraction-free state, tackle batches of difficult but important intellectual work.

  2. Embrace boredom.
  3. Quit social media.
  4. Drain the shallows.

    During your leisure time, do not let random matters capture your attention. Instead, actively think about how you should spend your day.

2. The Science of the Lazy Brain

Ego depletion: When our self-control is depleted, we are more likely to give in to everyday temptations. Scientists call this self-control fatigue “ego depletion.” If you want to change your behavior, make sure the goals you set are within a reasonable range.

Overcompensation: When we are tense, stressed, or in a bad mood, the brain seeks compensation, such as overeating or revenge shopping.

Duration rather than intensity: Our bodies do not need brief, high-intensity exercise; they need moderate-intensity training of sufficient duration.

Deep Work

  • Shallow work includes doing work you already know, frequently attending meetings and events, replying to emails, and so on. This kind of work only makes a person look busy; it creates no new value and is easy to replicate or replace.
    • Reasons:
      1. The principle of least resistance: everyone wants to avoid difficulty and choose simple tasks.
      2. Busyness is treated as a proxy for productivity.
      3. Internet-centrism: being consumed by online entertainment.
  1. Choose a mode:
    • Monastic mode: isolate yourself for deep work and cut off channels of contact with the outside world.
    • Rhythmic mode: do the same thing at a fixed time each day to establish a rhythm.
    • Bimodal mode: divide your time into two parts, reserving one explicitly for deep work and the other for shallow work. During deep-work periods, bimodal workers pursue intense, distraction-free concentration.
    • Journalistic mode: enter a state of deep work anytime and anywhere, shifting into it whenever free time appears.
  2. Internalize the habit and practice deliberately.
  3. Use quantitative metrics.
  4. Appropriately reduce working hours and meditate.

中文版

1.《深度工作》：深度工作准则

核心观点

  1. 深度工作是有价值的。
  2. 深度工作是少见的。
  3. 深度工作是有意义的。

准则

  1. 工作要深入。

    在长时间无干扰的状态下，批量解决困难却重要的智力工作。

  2. 拥抱无聊。
  3. 远离社交媒体。
  4. 摒弃浮浅。

    在个人娱乐的时候，不要被随意的事务吸引，而是主动思考我该如何度过一天。

2.《懒惰脑科学》

自我损耗：当我们的自控力缺失时，更容易经不住日常诱惑。科学家把这种自控力疲劳命名为“自我损耗”。如果你希望改变自己的行为，就要确保自己制定的目标在合理范围内。

过度补偿：当我们精神紧张、压力大或心情不好时，大脑会寻找补偿，比如大吃大喝或者报复性购物。

时长而非强度：我们的身体并不需要短时间、高强度的体育锻炼，而是需要达到一定时长的中等强度训练。

深度工作

  • 浮浅工作包括做自己已经熟悉的工作、频繁参加各类会议活动、回复邮件等等。这类工作只会让一个人看起来很忙，并不会创造新价值，且容易被复制和替换。
    • 原因：
      1. 最小阻力原则：人人都想避免困难，选择简单的任务。
      2. 忙碌代表生产力。
      3. 网络中心主义：被网络娱乐吞噬。
  1. 选择模式：
    • 禁欲模式：与外界隔绝，进行深度工作，切断与外界联系的媒介。
    • 节奏模式：每天在某个固定时间做同一件事，形成节奏。
    • 双峰模式：将时间分成两块，一块明确用于深度工作，另一块用于浮浅工作。在深度工作的时间里，双峰工作者会追求高强度、无干扰的专注。
    • 记者模式：随时随地进入深度工作状态，一有空闲时间就进入深度工作状态。
  2. 内化习惯，刻意练习。
  3. 使用量化指标。
  4. 适当减少工作时间并进行冥想。
