Choosing ETFs: VAS, VGS, and Other Options

English Version

Share-Market Terms

  • The ex-entitlement date is the date on which an entitlement adjustment takes effect. Investors who buy on or after this date are not entitled to the upcoming dividend or other benefit.
  • Franking credits represent Australian company tax already paid on profits distributed as dividends. Shareholders include the grossed-up dividend in taxable income, claim the franking credit, and ultimately pay tax according to their marginal tax rate.
  • A hedged ETF invests in overseas assets while reducing exposure to exchange-rate fluctuations. It may suit investors who want international exposure with less currency risk.
  • Dividend timeline: declaration date → ex-dividend date (shares must generally be bought before this date to receive the dividend) → record date → payment date.

What to Consider When Choosing a Fund

  • The size of the fund manager and the fund itself.
  • The annual management fee.
  • The index tracked and the top 10 holdings.
  • Five- and ten-year performance and returns.
  • The effect of exchange-rate movements.
  • Avoid home-country bias by investing globally and diversifying risk.

VAS

  • Tracks the S&P/ASX 300 Index and provides exposure to the Australian market.
  • Review its top 10 companies and its substantial exposure to financials and materials.
  • Review recent performance.
  • Review distribution history, including past amounts and frequency, such as quarterly or half-yearly.

VGS

  • Tracks the MSCI World ex-Australia Index and invests in many of the world’s largest and best-known companies.
  • The MSCI World ex-Australia Index, compiled by MSCI, tracks developed share markets worldwide excluding Australia.
  • Combining Australian and international exposure can reduce home-country bias and dependence on the local economy.

Source: https://www.vanguard.com.au/adviser/learn/vas-and-vgs

VGAD

  • Vanguard MSCI Index International Shares (Hedged) ETF (VGAD) is the currency-hedged counterpart to VGS.
  • Hedging reduces, but may not completely eliminate, the effect of currency movements on returns.
  • VGAD versus VGS:
    • A rising Australian dollar generally weighs on the unhedged VGS return when overseas assets are translated into AUD.
    • One market-timing view is to prefer VGAD when the Australian dollar is low and VGS when it is high. However, future exchange rates are uncertain, so this is not a reliable rule.
    • Reference: https://sydneyuberer.com/vgad-vanguard-hedged-etf-vgad-vs-vgs/

S&P 500

  • Intended here as a long-term investment held for more than 10 years.
  • It is a capitalisation-weighted index, so larger companies receive larger weights.
  • Its constituents are updated over time. Historically, it has produced strong long-term returns, but it is the market benchmark rather than something that “beats the market.”
  • Examples:
    • Vanguard S&P 500 ETF: VOO
    • iShares Core S&P 500 ETF: IVV
    • SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust: SPY
  • Share markets do not grow in a straight line: they may make money over long periods while remaining highly volatile in the short term.

VGE: Emerging-Markets ETF

  • Vanguard FTSE Emerging Markets Shares ETF (AUD).
  • Emerging-market funds generally invest in developing countries and regions such as China, India, Brazil, Russia, South Africa, and Mexico, although sanctions and index rules may restrict current access to some markets, including Russia. These markets may offer higher growth potential and market dynamism but also involve greater risk and volatility.

VTS

  • Vanguard U.S. Total Market Shares Index ETF.

VEU

  • Vanguard All-World ex-U.S. Shares Index ETF (VEU).

VDHG: Fund of Funds

  • A diversified fund that holds a mix of ETFs.
  • Simple and efficient, with returns broadly reflecting its diversified asset mix; it may not suit someone seeking especially high risk and return.
  • Diversifies risk and automatically rebalances assets, effectively buying low and selling high.
  • Compared with BetaShares DHHF, an all-in-one investment solution allocated 100% to shares, VDHG also includes defensive assets.

DHHF

  • A BetaShares diversified all-growth ETF and all-in-one portfolio allocated to growth assets.

NDQ

  • An ETF providing exposure to the Nasdaq-100 Index.

VEU.AX

  • The Australian Securities Exchange listing of VEU.

Lump-Sum Investing vs Regular Instalments

  • If a large sum is already available, it can be invested as a lump sum rather than split into instalments. This provides earlier market exposure, although staged investing may reduce timing anxiety.

VanEck MVW

  • Primarily covers the Australian market using an equal-weight strategy.

VanEck QUAL

  • Has substantial U.S. exposure—about 70% as noted—plus exposure to other countries.

VanEck EMKT

  • Provides exposure to emerging and developing markets.

中文版

股票市场术语

  • 除权日（Ex-entitlement date）是权益调整生效的日期。在这一天或之后买入股票的投资者，无权获得即将发放的股息或其他权益。
  • 股息抵税额（Franking credits）代表澳大利亚公司在分配股息前已经缴纳的公司所得税。股东把还原后的股息计入应税收入，同时申报股息抵税额，最终按照自己的边际税率纳税。
  • 对冲 ETF 投资海外资产，同时降低汇率波动带来的风险。它适合希望获得国际资产敞口、但想减少汇率风险的投资者。
  • 股息时间线：宣布日 → 除息日（通常必须在该日期之前买入股票才有权获得股息）→ 登记日 → 支付日。

选择基金时的注意事项

  • 基金公司和基金本身的规模。
  • 每年的管理费。
  • 跟踪的指数和前十大持仓。
  • 五年和十年的表现与回报。
  • 汇率波动的影响。
  • 打破本国偏好，通过投资全球市场来分散风险。

VAS

  • 跟踪 S&P/ASX 300 指数，提供澳大利亚市场敞口。
  • 查看前十大公司，以及基金在金融和材料行业中所占的较大比重。
  • 查看近几年的表现。
  • 查看历史分配，包括过去的金额及频率，例如每季度或每半年一次。

VGS

  • 跟踪 MSCI World ex-Australia 指数，投资于许多全球规模最大、知名度最高的公司。
  • MSCI World ex-Australia 指数由 MSCI 编制，跟踪除澳大利亚以外全球发达国家股票市场的表现。
  • 同时配置澳大利亚与国际市场，可以减少本国偏好以及对本地经济的依赖。

来源：https://www.vanguard.com.au/adviser/learn/vas-and-vgs

VGAD

  • Vanguard MSCI Index International Shares (Hedged) ETF（VGAD）是 VGS 的货币对冲版本。
  • 对冲可以降低汇率波动对回报的影响，但未必能将其完全消除。
  • VGAD 与 VGS：
    • 澳元升值时，海外资产换算为澳元后，通常会对未对冲的 VGS 回报产生负面影响。
    • 一种择时观点是：澳元处于低位时选择 VGAD，澳元处于高位时选择 VGS。不过，未来汇率无法确定，因此这并不是可靠的规则。
    • 参考：https://sydneyuberer.com/vgad-vanguard-hedged-etf-vgad-vs-vgs/

S&P 500（标普 500）

  • 这里把它视为持有 10 年以上的长期投资。
  • 它是市值加权指数，市值越大的公司权重越高。
  • 指数成分会持续更新。它过去取得了强劲的长期回报，但它本身是市场基准，不能称为“跑赢大盘”。
  • 示例：
    • Vanguard S&P 500 ETF：VOO
    • iShares Core S&P 500 ETF：IVV
    • SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust：SPY
  • 股票市场不会线性增长：长期可能赚钱，短期波动却可能很大。

VGE：新兴市场 ETF

  • Vanguard FTSE Emerging Markets Shares ETF（澳元）。
  • 新兴市场基金通常投资于中国、印度、巴西、俄罗斯、南非、墨西哥等发展中国家和地区，不过制裁和指数规则目前可能限制对包括俄罗斯在内的部分市场的投资。这些市场可能拥有较高的经济增长潜力和市场活力，但风险与波动也更高。

VTS

  • Vanguard U.S. Total Market Shares Index ETF。

VEU

  • Vanguard All-World ex-U.S. Shares Index ETF（VEU）。

VDHG：基金中的基金

  • 持有多种 ETF 的分散型基金。
  • 简单高效，回报大致反映其分散化资产组合；可能不适合特别追求高风险和高回报的人。
  • 分散风险并自动进行资产再平衡，相当于低买高卖。
  • 相比 BetaShares DHHF 这种 100% 配置股票的一站式投资方案，VDHG 还包含防御型资产。

DHHF

  • BetaShares 的分散型全成长 ETF，是配置成长型资产的一站式投资组合。

NDQ

  • 提供 Nasdaq-100 指数敞口的 ETF。

VEU.AX

  • VEU 在澳大利亚证券交易所的上市代码。

一次性投入与分批定投

  • 如果手中已经有一大笔资金，可以一次性投入，而不必拆成多次。这能让资金更早进入市场，但分批投入可能减轻择时焦虑。

VanEck MVW

  • 主要覆盖澳大利亚市场，采用等权重策略。

VanEck QUAL

  • 大量投资于美国——笔记记录约为 70%——同时也投资其他国家。

VanEck EMKT

  • 提供新兴市场和发展中国家的投资敞口。
