A Night in Bali: I Couldn’t Find Anyone to Join Me, So I Went to the Bar Alone

That evening, I tried to find someone to go to a bar with, but nobody was available.

So I went alone.

When I arrived, I noticed a woman sitting at a table by herself and started talking to her. I think she was from Indonesia or Malaysia. She was beautiful and very sweet. We eventually went dancing together and stayed on the dance floor from 10 p.m. until 1 a.m.

At one point, I thought, “This might be the happiest thing in the world.”

There were lights, music, and a crowd of people temporarily forgetting about tomorrow. For a moment, it felt like paradise.

After dancing for a while, though, I began to wonder whether losing yourself in this kind of pleasure for too long could also become empty.

Some of the men became overconfident and started moving too close, so I no longer wanted to dance. Many people were also smoking in the middle of the crowd. The smell was overwhelming, and smoking in such a packed space felt inconsiderate.

The happiness was real, but so was the discomfort.

Travelling does not mean I have to ignore my boundaries just to fit in. I can walk into a bar alone, introduce myself to a stranger, and still leave when something no longer feels right.

Solo travel does not mean doing every single thing alone.

Sometimes you only need enough courage to walk out the door, and the world will lend you someone to dance with for the night.

中文版

晚上，我想找人一起去酒吧，但没有找到。

那就自己去吧。

到了以后，我正好看到一个女生独自坐了一桌，于是过去和她聊天。她好像来自印度尼西亚或马来西亚，长得很好看，人也很可爱。后来我们一起去跳舞，从晚上十点一直跳到凌晨一点。

跳舞的时候，我突然觉得：这好像就是世界上最快乐的事情。

灯光、音乐、人群，所有人都暂时忘记明天，好像天堂也不过如此。

但跳久了，我又开始想：如果一直沉浸在这种醉生梦死的快乐里，会不会也没有那么好？

后来有些男生开始得意忘形，贴得越来越近，我就不想跳了。这里还有很多人一边跳舞一边抽烟，烟味很重。我不喜欢这种感觉，也觉得在拥挤的人群里抽烟很没有礼貌。

快乐是真的，但不舒服也是真的。

旅行并不会要求我为了“融入”而忽略自己的边界。我可以一个人走进酒吧，可以主动认识陌生人，也可以在不舒服的时候转身离开。

一个人旅行并不代表每件事都要独自完成。

有时候，你只是鼓起勇气出了门，世界就会临时送给你一个一起跳舞的人。
